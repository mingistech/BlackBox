# BlackBox 1.3

BlackBox 1.3 adds OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol while keeping GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol available, so you can choose which generation works best for your tasks. Existing selections and favorites are preserved.

The recommended OpenRouter list includes both Astra generations, and the full compatible model catalog remains available.

## Installation

Requires macOS 26.6 or later. The universal app supports Apple silicon and Intel, and is signed and notarized by Apple. Download the ZIP, unzip it, and move BlackBox to Applications or your external drive. API access and billing depend on your provider.

If you use portable keys, keep `BlackBox.keys` beside the app when replacing it.
